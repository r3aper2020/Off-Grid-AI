## Neosporin Original ointment (triple antibiotic): active ingredient, purpose and uses
- Active ingredients (in each gram) — Purpose
- Bacitracin Zinc (400 units) — First aid antibiotic
- Neomycin Sulfate (3.5 mg) — First aid antibiotic
- Polymyxin B sulfate (5,000 units) — First aid antibiotic

**Use**
first aid to help prevent infection in minor:
- cuts
- scrapes
- burns

## Neosporin Original ointment (triple antibiotic): warnings
**Warnings**
For external use only.

**Do not use**
- if you are allergic to any of the ingredients
- in the eyes
- over large areas of the body

**Ask a doctor before use if you have**
- deep or puncture wounds
- animal bites
- serious burns

**Stop use and ask a doctor if**
- you need to use longer than 1 week
- condition persists or gets worse
- rash or other allergic reaction develops

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Neosporin Original ointment (triple antibiotic): directions (dosing)
**Directions**
- clean the affected area
- apply a small amount of this product (an amount equal to the surface area of the tip of a finger) on the area 1 to 3 times daily
- may be covered with a sterile bandage

## Neosporin Original ointment (triple antibiotic): other information
**Other information**
- store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77° F)
