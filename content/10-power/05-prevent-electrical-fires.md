# Prevent Home Electrical Fires (U.S. Fire Administration)
Source: https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/publications/prevent_home_electrical_fires_flyer.pdf

## Prevent Home Electrical Fires

Electrical malfunction is a leading cause of home fires year after year.

Share these electrical fire safety tips in your community:

- Electrical work should only be done by a qualified electrician.

- Check your electrical cords. If they are cracked or damaged, replace them. Don’t try to repair them.

- Don’t overload extension cords or wall outlets.

- Never use extension cords with appliances. Plug them directly into wall outlets.
